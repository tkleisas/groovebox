# NSR-1 footprints

`SW_Hotswap_Kailh_MX_Plated_1.00u.kicad_mod` is vendored from
[kiswitch/keyswitch-kicad-library](https://github.com/kiswitch/keyswitch-kicad-library),
`library/footprints/Switch_Keyboard_Hotswap_Kailh.pretty`, upgraded to the
KiCad 10 footprint format, and stripped of its courtyard because the in-switch
LED footprints intentionally sit inside the switch body. License: CC-BY-SA 4.0
(see `LICENSE-CC-BY-SA`).

`DIN5-RA-PTH.kicad_mod` is a vendored SparkFun-Connectors footprint,
converted to the KiCad 10 format. `SW_SPDT_SLIDE_THT.kicad_mod` is a generic
EG1218-class SPDT slide footprint, generated for the NSR-2 power switch and
reused here for the NSR-1 power board.
